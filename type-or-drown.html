// HOW TO PLAY //

  1. 01A verb appears on screen. Type its Past Simple and Past Participle forms in the letter boxes.
  2. 02Press ENTER or click CONFIRM ANSWER to submit. Correct answers pump water out of the tank.
  3. 03A wrong answer causes a water burst (+10% water level). Fix the red boxes and try again.
  4. 04Conjugate 6 verbs correctly per level to win. Don't let the water reach 100% or it's Game Over!
  5. 05Each level gets faster. Levels 1–3 use regular verbs, 4–7 common irregulars, 8–10 advanced irregulars.
